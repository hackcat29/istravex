<Update label="v1.3.0" description="18 November 2026">
  Every XDR shape real traffic uses, seven SDKs, a sweep over real transactions, and seven new defects.

  **Corpus**
  - [`shape-scan`](/corpus/cell-coverage) records the XDR cells (union arms, flag bits, contract cost types) every transaction and ledger exercises; scans of 400,311 mainnet and testnet ledgers (59.6 million transactions) are committed in `corpus/scan/`
  - 116 fixtures chosen by set cover cover all 470 cells the networks use, with the generic `tx` and `ledger` profiles: bytes from the canonical ledger, expected values from Horizon. 137 fixtures in all: 95 mainnet, 30 testnet
  - [CAP coverage](/corpus/cap-coverage) by cells: 45 of the 47 live CAPs with a ledger shape, every other CAP with the reason it leaves none; [`corpus/cells.json`](/corpus/cell-coverage)
  - The testnet data lake, byte-stable unlike RPC, and protocol 30 shapes from it: CAP-0084 muxed contract addresses and CAP-0088 millisecond close times

  **Conformance**
  - Python `stellar-xdr` 0.2.1 and `stellar-php-sdk` 1.18.0 join JavaScript, Go, Rust, Python `stellar-sdk` and Java: seven SDKs, 2,810/2,816 checks, every failure a finding
  - [The sweep](/tools/sdk-runners#sweeping-real-traffic): Go, Rust, JavaScript, Python and Java over 1,241 whole ledgers, each round-tripped byte for byte and each of 310,837 transaction hashes matched against stellar-core's

  **Pilots**
  - wallet-backend: 123/123, over RPC and the data lake; stellar-etl v2.8.34: 119/123, the misses still its open #454 and #460

  **Findings**
  - Seven new defects: F-0023 (java-stellar-sdk) and F-0024 (stellar-php-sdk) compute the wrong hash for transactions whose `PRECOND_V2` fields are all defaults; F-0021, PHP muxed IDs above 2⁶³ come back negative; F-0022, PHP decodes past XDR maximums; drafted for filing. F-0020: js-stellar-sdk before 17.3.0 can't decode protocol 30 XDR
  - [RFP sightings](/corpus/sightings): 13 of 18 reproduced before and after their fixes, 3 exercised, 2 out of reach with the reason; new reproductions for rs-stellar-xdr #581 and #598, rs-soroban-sdk #2099 and js-xdr #165
  - Apache-2.0 `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files
</Update>

<Update label="v1.2.0" description="15 November 2026">
  Mainnet-first, five SDKs, a second upstream indexer, and a security policy.

  **Mainnet**
  - Generated fixtures are built at protocol 29, mainnet's; CI also generates, verifies and runs the whole corpus at protocol 30
  - [`mainnet-scan`](/corpus/capturing#finding-the-transactions) searches SDF's public data lake for every corpus pattern; its first run covered 400,000 ledgers
  - Four new mainnet fixtures from it: a muxed ID above 2⁶³, a USDC trustline created through the SAC, a depth-4 authorization tree, and the 100-operation and 20-signature transactions
  - A new pattern, the largest amount (i64::MAX stroops): 20 fixtures across 14 patterns, 6 of them from mainnet

  **Conformance**
  - Python `stellar-sdk` 17.0.1 and `java-stellar-sdk` 6.0.0 join JavaScript, Go and Rust: 278/280 checks. The two failures are known upstream bugs, py-stellar-base#1250 and java-stellar-sdk#832

  **Pilots**
  - [stellar-etl](/pilots/stellar-etl) v2.8.34, through its own transforms: 7/11 correct. The four misses are its open issues #454 (float amounts) and #460 (no effect for a SAC `trust()`), reproduced on mainnet data too
  - wallet-backend: 11/11

  **Findings and security**
  - 19 findings, 14 reproduced; each records how it was disclosed
  - A [security policy](/project/security): private vulnerability reporting, and private disclosure first for exploitable findings

  **Docs**
  - The docs domain is DNS-only, as Mintlify requires (it returned 403 while proxied)
</Update>

<Update label="v1.1.0" description="15 November 2026">
  Against the SCF RFP's reviewer checks: every check now runs in CI. See the [roadmap](/project/roadmap#against-the-rfps-reviewer-checks).

  **Corpus and generator**
  - 15 fixtures across 6 CAPs and 13 patterns (from 6): muxed source and the maximum muxed ID, a Soroban operation from a muxed source, pool issuers out of text order, map keys in host order, a six-level authorization tree, memo text that isn't UTF-8, every vector at its maximum, and two synthetic fixtures a decoder must refuse
  - The generator is byte-reproducible (seeded keys, fixed close times, a seeded settings-upgrade salt), takes any network passphrase and an older protocol, and `--check` proves it on every push, for all three passphrases
  - A probe contract for specific ScVal shapes and authorization trees, its Wasm rebuilt byte for byte in CI
  - Manifests gain `pattern`, `sightings`, `source.related` and `expect: decode_error`; CAP coverage 5 of 40

  **Replay and pilot**
  - The corpus in the Galexie data-lake layout, byte-identical to SDF's public lake for pubnet, with a read-only S3 endpoint
  - wallet-backend runs over RPC and over its datastore backend: 8/8 both ways
  - wallet-backend #729, #731 and #716 reproduce on the commits before their fixes and pass on the fixes; persistence encoding is checked without a database
  - The mock RPC serves a 542 MB corpus under a 128 MB heap: header-only decoding and a 16 MiB page budget

  **Conformance**
  - Named native checks for every pattern, in JavaScript, Go and Rust
  - JavaScript runs four releases: 17.2.0 and 17.2.1 fail exactly the checks for js-stellar-sdk #1766, #1785 and #1789; 17.3.0 and 17.4.0 pass

  **Linter**
  - IX001 also flags muxed IDs converted to a lossy type; IX002 also requires size limits and catches work lists; IX003 also flags a muxed account's `M…` form as a participant
  - Calibrated on wallet-backend: clean on main, firing on the commits before #753 (IX003) and 9538b3c (IX002); the self-test seeds six violations

  **Guide**
  - New: [What stellar-core validates](/guide/validated-fields) (field by field, checked against the corpus in CI), [Recommended limits](/guide/limits), [Addresses and strkeys](/guide/addresses); rules 1 and 8 updated
  - Exports as Docusaurus Markdown for developers.stellar.org

  **Findings**
  - [`corpus/findings.json`](/project/findings): 15 findings with severity, upstream issue, corpus entry and the checks they explain; CI fails on any reproduced failure without one. Served at `/v1/findings`. Three new ones filed upstream: stellar-core#5507 and #5508, js-stellar-sdk#1906

  **Hosting**
  - No request logs: Cloudflare Workers observability and nginx access logs are off
</Update>
