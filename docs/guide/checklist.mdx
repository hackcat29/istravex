Copy this into your pull request template, or walk through it before shipping an ingestion change.

- [ ] Effects, participants and balances are derived from ledger metadata, not the envelope. → [Rule 1](/guide/effects-from-meta)
- [ ] The fee payer comes from the fee-bump fee account; the fee amount from `TransactionResult.FeeCharged`. → [Rule 2](/guide/fees)
- [ ] Balance-delta fee accounting nets Soroban post-apply refunds. → [Rule 2](/guide/fees)
- [ ] Every 64-bit integer is stored as a string or `NUMERIC`, and emitted as a JSON string. → [Rule 3](/guide/wide-integers)
- [ ] Muxed IDs are kept alongside the underlying account; fee-bump-wrapped operations are handled. → [Rule 4](/guide/muxed-accounts)
- [ ] Signed-payload signers are stored as full `P…` keys. → [Rule 5](/guide/signed-payload-signers)
- [ ] Trustlines and accounts created by contract invocations are indexed from meta. → [Rule 6](/guide/sac-trustlines)
- [ ] Memo text is UTF-8 validated or stored as bytes. → [Rule 7](/guide/untrusted-strings)
- [ ] Recursive XDR walkers carry and check a depth limit. → [Rule 8](/guide/bounded-recursion)
- [ ] Diagnostic events are never indexed, hashed or compared across nodes. → [Rule 9](/guide/consensus-data)
- [ ] The Istravex corpus replays through your pipeline with every fixture correct. → [Replaying into your indexer](/pilots/your-indexer)
- [ ] `istravex-lint ./...` is clean, or every finding is triaged with a reason. → [istravex-lint](/tools/lint)
